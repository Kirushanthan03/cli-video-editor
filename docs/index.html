🚀 v1.0 Production Ready

CLI Video Editor

Turn raw footage into viral, broadcast-quality 9:16 vertical reels with frame-accurate subtitles and procedural motion graphics.

⚡ Quick Start (1-Minute Guide)

Drop your talking-head camera video into raw/ and any reference B-roll videos into reference/:

Option A: Auto-Detect (Single or Multiple References)

python3 pipeline/auto_editor.py --raw raw/my_video.mp4 --theme cyber_neon --speed 1.18

Option B: Explicit Multi-Reference Paths

python3 pipeline/auto_editor.py --raw raw/my_talk.mp4 --ref reference/app_demo.mp4 reference/benchmark.mp4 reference/code.mp4

✨ What happens: The engine extracts the best visual cuts from every reference video, strips reference audio 100%, and floats the creator in a glowing Picture-in-Picture (PiP) bubble during cuts.

01. How the Pipeline Works

1

Silence Detection

Automatically detects and trims leading dead air (before speaking) and trailing camera reach.

2

Speech-to-Text

Uses faster-whisper for multi-language transcription (Tamil, English, Hindi) with millisecond timestamps.

3

Speed Compensation

Preserves 100% of spoken words at 1.18x speed with pitch-preserved audio and synchronized subtitles.

4

Visual Director

Detects keywords, stats, and benchmarks in speech to plan animated kinetic stickers and overlays.

5

Procedural Motion

Renders transparent vector cards, hand-drawn doodles, and badges with spring physics.

6

Master Assembly

Composites full HD 1080x1920 video, ASS subtitles, procedural UI sound effects, and -14 LUFS audio.

03. Subtitles & Studio Audio

All subtitles automatically follow the proven Social Reel Preset (presets/subtitles/social_reel_preset.ass) with strict 5.5px contrast outlines and vertical safe-zone positioning:

THE ALL-NEW GEMINI 4 NEON SCORES 89.1%
  • White Text: Standard conversational speech
  • Yellow Accent: Core tech entities, model names, and action verbs
  • Green Accent: Benchmark metrics, speeds, and price advantages
  • Clean Audio Guarantee: Leveled to broadcast EBU R128 (-14 LUFS) with zero background narrator chatter.

04. CLI Options Cheatsheet

Flag Description Example
--raw / -i Path to input raw footage --raw raw/test.mp4
--speed / -s Playback speed multiplier --speed 1.15
--theme / -t Motion graphics theme --theme apple_keynote
--dry-run Audit speech & plan scenes without rendering --dry-run
--no-overlays Subtitles & voice master only (no cards) --no-overlays
